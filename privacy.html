Privacy Policy

Effective November 6, 2026

This policy explains what information Magisym Software LLC ("we", "us") collects in the Intuition Games app (the "App"), how we use it and the choices you have.

We keep data collection to what the App needs to work and provide its features.

Information we collect

Information you give us

  • Player name and avatar you choose. These are shown to other players in battles, friends lists and leaderboards.
  • Email address, only if you choose to sign in to save your progress. We use it to send you a sign-in code and to identify your account.
  • Messages you send us, such as support emails.
  • Reports you send about other players, including the reason you chose, and records of players you block.

Information created when you play

  • A unique player ID created automatically on first launch, so the game server knows which progress is yours. This ID does not require your name or email address, but it is associated with your game activity.
  • Game activity: scores, streaks, match results, friends, card collection, familiars, Moon Coin balance and whether you have unlocked the full version.
  • Saved tarot readings and other progress information that the App stores for you. If you sign in, your saved progress may be backed up to your account so you can restore it on another device.

Technical information

When the App connects to our servers, we and our service providers may automatically process limited technical information needed to operate and secure the App. This may include IP addresses, device type, operating system, connection times, authentication records and error or security logs.

We use this information to maintain the App, troubleshoot problems, prevent cheating and protect accounts. We do not use it to track you across other companies' apps or websites for advertising.

Purchases

Purchases are processed by Apple or Google. We never see or store your payment card details.

We receive purchase-related information, such as what was purchased, transaction identifiers and purchase status, so we can deliver and restore your purchases.

We use RevenueCat to manage and verify purchases. RevenueCat may process purchase records, app-user identifiers and limited device information as part of providing this service.

What we don't collect

We don't request access to your precise GPS location, contacts or personal photos to operate the App.

The App has no advertising, and we don't use your device's advertising ID to track you across other companies' apps or websites.

We don't sell your personal information or share it for targeted advertising.

Some technical information, such as an IP address, may reveal an approximate geographic area to the service providers that process it. This is different from collecting your precise device location.

How we use information

We use information:

  • To run the App, including saving progress, online battles, friends and leaderboards
  • To deliver, verify and restore purchases
  • To prevent cheating, investigate abuse and keep the App secure, including reviewing player reports
  • To respond to support requests
  • To troubleshoot technical problems and maintain the App
  • To comply with applicable legal obligations

Where applicable privacy laws require a legal basis for processing, we generally process information to provide the App and its features, protect our services and players, comply with legal obligations, or with your consent where required.

Daily reminders are scheduled on your device. You can turn them off anytime in your device's notification settings.

Information shared with other players

Your chosen player name and avatar may be visible to other players in battles, friends lists and leaderboards. Other gameplay information, such as scores and results, may also be visible where those features require it.

Your email address, purchase records and private account information are not displayed to other players.

Please avoid putting personal information in your player name that you don't want other people to see.

Service providers

We share information only as needed with providers that help us operate the App:

  • Supabase: Database, account sign-in, player progress and game server functions.
  • RevenueCat: Purchase management, verification and restoration.
  • Apple and Google: App distribution, purchases and related platform services.

These providers may process information on our behalf or under their own applicable terms and privacy policies. We require service providers acting on our behalf to handle information under appropriate privacy and security obligations.

We may also disclose information if required by law or reasonably necessary to investigate fraud, protect players, or defend our legal rights.

We do not sell player information to advertisers or data brokers.

Data security

We take reasonable measures to protect the information we maintain, including using established service providers and access controls.

However, no online service or method of storing information is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

If you believe your account has been accessed without permission, please contact us.

Data retention and deletion

We generally keep your account information and game progress while your account is active and for as long as needed to provide the App.

You can delete your account at any time in the App (Profile → Delete account), or contact us at support@magisym.com for assistance.

Deleting your account removes your player profile, cards, familiars, saved progress and Moon Coins from our active account systems. Past match results involving other players are kept without your name or picture, shown as "Deleted player." This applies to guest accounts as well as accounts connected to an email address.

Deleting the App from your device does not necessarily delete your guest account or its information from our servers. To delete that information, use the account deletion option in Profile before uninstalling the App.

Some limited information may need to be retained after deletion, including purchase records required by law, records needed to investigate fraud or abuse, and information contained in temporary system backups. Such information is retained only as long as reasonably necessary for those purposes or as required by law.

Deletion of backup copies may take additional time as backups are replaced or removed under the applicable retention procedures.

Once your account and its associated progress are deleted, we generally cannot restore your cards, familiars, Moon Coins or saved readings. Certain non-consumable purchases may still be restorable through the original Apple or Google store account, as explained in our Terms of Service.

You may also request deletion or access to your information by contacting support@magisym.com.

Children

The App is intended for users aged 13 and older. It is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

If we learn that we have collected personal information from a child under 13, we will take steps to delete it in accordance with applicable law.

If you believe a child under 13 has used the App or provided us with information, please contact us at support@magisym.com.

Additional privacy protections or consent requirements may apply to younger users depending on where they live.

Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • Access information we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate account information
  • Request deletion of your information
  • Receive a copy of certain information, where required by law
  • Object to or restrict certain uses of your information, where applicable
  • Withdraw consent for processing that depends on your consent

You can change your player name and certain preferences within the App. You can also turn off notifications through your device's settings.

To make a privacy request or ask a question about your information, email support@magisym.com. We may need to verify that the account belongs to you before fulfilling a request.

We will respond in accordance with applicable law. You may also have the right to contact your local data protection authority.

International data processing

Magisym Software LLC is based in the United States.

Our service providers may process or store information in the United States or other countries where they operate. Privacy laws may differ between those locations.

Where required, we will use appropriate safeguards for international transfers of personal information.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. We'll post the new version here and change the effective date above.

If we make significant changes, we'll try to notify you through the App or by another reasonable method. Where required by law, we will obtain your consent before making changes to how we use your information.
