Common questions

How do I restore my purchase?

In the app, open the Full Version screen and tap Restore purchase. Make sure you're signed in to the same Apple ID or Google account you bought with.

How do I keep my progress on a new phone?

On your Profile, tap Sign in with email. Your stats, cards and Moon Coins are saved to your account, so you can sign in on another device and pick up where you left off.

How do I delete my account?

On your Profile, sign in and tap Delete account. This permanently removes your account, stats, cards and Moon Coins. You can also email us and we'll delete it for you.

How do I play with a friend?

Open Gold Card Battle, tap Play a Friend and share your code. Your friend opens Battle, taps Join and enters it.
